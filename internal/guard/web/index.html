INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

Export the evidence, not a green badge.

A bundle contains the signed actions, replayable transitions and a signed RFC 6962 Merkle checkpoint. Verify it with a separately pinned audit key. A retained witness checkpoint detects rollback or a conflicting history.

kig verify --bundle evidence.json --trust audit-public.json --audience keyguard
kig witness --bundle evidence.json --trust audit-public.json \
  --witness-key witness.json --state witness-state.json

The log attests to the executor's reported history. It does not prove a compromised executor acted truthfully. Export does not imply independent witnessing.

LOCAL SIGNING

Prepare locally. Submit explicitly.

Use the CLI to obtain a bound challenge and sign a request. Add your deployment’s --url and --ca options. Load that request here within its expiry. The browser never needs your private key.

kig prepare --key writer.json --action resource.transfer \
  --resource treasury --version 1 \
  --body '{"to":"reserve","amount":25,"to_version":1}' --out request.json

Incident response commands

kig act --key admin.json --action key.revoke --resource KEY_FINGERPRINT --body '{}' --out revoke-request.json
kig act --key admin.json --action subject.quarantine --resource writer --body '{}' --out quarantine-request.json
kig act --key admin.json --action subject.role --resource writer --body '{"role":"auditor"}' --out role-request.json

Recovery authority keys are protected from routine revocation. Rotate them through a reviewed re-bootstrap procedure; the service does not invent a self-service root-of-trust reset.
